One image summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 — four stories, four regions,
four fields:

1. **Trump, Xi & Putin meet** — the first-ever trilateral summit opens in
   Shenzhen, on the sidelines of APEC.
2. **Anthropic's $81.3bn IPO** — the biggest stock-market debut of all time;
   shares begin trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday.
3. **Crimean Bridge hit again** — a third attack in a week halts traffic over
   the Kerch Strait crossing.
4. **GTA VI rolls out worldwide** — after a 13-year wait, the game launches at
   midnight local time around the world.

The four pictures are editorial illustrations generated for this entry; every
headline and caption is rendered in the image itself, so the date and the day's
events are readable at a glance. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only)
copies `entry.png` to the output directory.
